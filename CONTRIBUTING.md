# Contributing to SnowcrashAI

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the SnowcrashAI Foundation.

SnowcrashAI is a practitioner-led initiative focused on advancing AI security
assessment, architecture, and defensive understanding. Contributions are welcome,
but are intentionally guided to preserve clarity, scope, and credibility.

Please read this document before opening an issue or pull request.

---

## Guiding Principles

SnowcrashAI values contributions that are:

- **Practitioner-focused** — grounded in real-world experience
- **Defensible** — technically sound and reviewable
- **Scoped** — aligned with SnowcrashAI’s defined mission
- **Vendor-neutral** — no product or platform promotion
- **Non-weaponized** — no exploit payloads or step-by-step attacks

This project is not a certification framework, exploit library, or compliance checklist.

---

## What You Can Contribute

We welcome contributions in the following areas:

- Documentation clarity and improvements
- Methodology refinements
- Playbook enhancements (behavioral, not exploit-based)
- Risk taxonomy discussion and evolution
- Reference architectures (defensive and illustrative)
- Governance and process improvements

If you’re unsure whether an idea fits, open an issue first.

---

## What We Do Not Accept

Maintainers may decline contributions that:

- Include exploit payloads or attack scripts
- Attempt exhaustive or “complete” coverage
- Introduce compliance guarantees or certification claims
- Prescribe specific vendor tools or implementations
- Expand scope beyond AI security assessment and defense
- Blur the line between assessment methodology and red-team exploitation

Declining a contribution is not a judgment of quality or intent.

---

## How to Contribute

### 1. Open an Issue (Recommended)
For substantial changes, start with an issue describing:
- The problem you are addressing
- Why it matters to AI security practitioners
- How it aligns with SnowcrashAI’s scope

This helps avoid rework and misalignment.

---

### 2. Submit a Pull Request
When submitting a pull request:
- Keep changes focused and scoped
- Reference any related issues
- Explain *why* the change is needed, not just *what* changed
- Avoid introducing new terminology without justification

---

## Review and Decision Process

- Contributions are reviewed by SnowcrashAI maintainers
- Consensus is preferred, but not required
- Maintainers may request revisions or clarification
- Final decisions rest with the Lead Maintainer, as defined in governance

There is no voting system.

---

## Maintainer Authority

SnowcrashAI uses an explicit maintainer model.

Maintainers are responsible for:
- Technical direction
- Scope control
- Quality and consistency

Governance details are defined in:
`GOVERNANCE/GOV-2-maintainers-and-review.md`

---

## Code of Conduct

SnowcrashAI expects professional, respectful collaboration.

Security discussions can involve disagreement.  
Personal attacks, harassment, or bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

---

## Final Note

SnowcrashAI prioritizes **clarity over completeness** and **quality over quantity**.

If your contribution helps practitioners think more clearly about AI security,
it is likely a good fit.

Thank you for helping shape the project.
