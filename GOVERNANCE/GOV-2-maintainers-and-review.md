# GOV-2 — Maintainer Roles and Contribution Review Process

**Status:** Draft  
**Applies to:** SnowcrashAI Foundation  
**Version:** v0.1  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-24  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how decisions are made, how contributions are reviewed, and how maintainer authority is exercised within the SnowcrashAI Foundation.

The goal of governance in SnowcrashAI is to:

- Preserve technical integrity
- Prevent scope drift
- Enable practitioner-led contributions
- Maintain credibility without unnecessary bureaucracy

Governance exists to **protect the work**, not to slow it down.

---

## 2. Governance Principles

SnowcrashAI governance is guided by the following principles:

- **Practitioner-first:** Real-world applicability outweighs theoretical completeness  
- **Minimum necessary process:** Only the governance required to scale responsibly  
- **Explicit authority:** Decision ownership is always clear  
- **Transparency:** Decisions are documented and traceable  
- **Vendor neutrality:** No product, platform, or service preference  

---

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for the technical direction, quality, and integrity of SnowcrashAI artifacts.

**Maintainer responsibilities include:**
- Reviewing and approving contributions
- Resolving technical disagreements
- Ensuring alignment with SnowcrashAI principles
- Maintaining consistency across artifacts
- Participating in release readiness reviews

Maintainers are not required to implement all changes themselves.

---

### 3.2 Lead Maintainer

The Lead Maintainer has final decision authority when consensus cannot be reached.

**Lead Maintainer responsibilities:**
- Final arbitration on disputed changes
- Release approval and tagging
- Governance updates
- Roadmap alignment decisions

This role exists to avoid ambiguity, not to centralize power.

---

### 3.3 Contributors

Contributors may propose changes via issues or pull requests.

Contributors:
- Do not require prior approval to submit ideas
- Are encouraged to provide practitioner context
- Are expected to follow contribution guidelines

Contribution does not imply decision authority.

---

## 4. Decision Authority Model

SnowcrashAI follows an explicit maintainer decision model:

- **Consensus when possible**
- **Maintainer decision when necessary**
- **Lead Maintainer decision when blocked**

There is no voting system.

Decisions are evaluated based on:
- Alignment with SnowcrashAI scope
- Technical correctness
- Practitioner impact
- Long-term maintainability

---

## 5. Contribution Review Process

### 5.1 Contribution Types

Common contribution types include:
- Documentation improvements
- New or updated playbooks
- Risk taxonomy refinements
- Reference architectures
- Methodology clarifications

---

### 5.2 Review Flow

1. Contributor opens an issue or pull request  
2. Maintainer reviews for:
   - Scope alignment
   - Technical accuracy
   - Consistency with existing artifacts  
3. Maintainer may request revisions  
4. Maintainer approves or declines the contribution  

Not all contributions will be accepted. Rejection is not a reflection of contribution quality.

---

### 5.3 Acceptance Criteria

A contribution may be accepted if it:
- Aligns with SnowcrashAI scope
- Adds practitioner value
- Avoids vendor-specific bias
- Does not prematurely expand scope

---

## 6. Scope Control

SnowcrashAI intentionally limits scope.

Maintainers may decline contributions that:
- Attempt exhaustive coverage
- Introduce compliance guarantees
- Add production exploit payloads
- Create certification or assurance claims
- Over-prescribe implementation details

This is by design.

---

## 7. Release Governance

Releases are approved by the Lead Maintainer.

Release approval requires:
- Review of included artifacts
- Confirmation of scope adherence
- Documentation completeness

Pre-releases may be used to signal early availability.

---

## 8. Governance Evolution

This governance model may evolve as the project grows.

Changes to governance require:
- Maintainer discussion
- Lead Maintainer approval
- Documentation update

Governance changes are expected to be infrequent and deliberate.

---

## 9. Summary

SnowcrashAI governance exists to:
- Enable contribution
- Maintain credibility
- Protect practitioner value
- Avoid unnecessary process

Clarity over consensus. Quality over quantity.
